# RETech

A Factorio mod that extends the game's technology tree with new research facilities, advanced technologies, and specialized research categories. RETech is designed to complement the RExtended mod, providing the necessary research paths to unlock and utilize the advanced machines and production chains introduced in RExtended.

## Features

- **Advanced Research Facilities**: New laboratory types with improved research capabilities, enabling faster and more efficient research.
- **Extended Technology Tree**: New technologies across multiple categories, creating deeper and more complex research paths.
- **Specialized Research Categories**: Custom research categories for different aspects of the game, organizing technologies logically.
- **Integration with RExtended**: Seamless integration with the RExtended mod's machines and components, ensuring proper progression.
- **Cross-mod Compatibility**: Support for various mod categories and technologies, allowing for flexible mod combinations.
- **Progressive Research Paths**: Carefully balanced research requirements that create meaningful progression through the game.
- **Enhanced Visual Feedback**: Custom icons and graphics for all new technologies and research facilities.

## Research Facilities

### Laboratories

#### Basic Laboratory (R1)
- Standard research facility with basic capabilities
- Processes all science packs
- Standard research speed and power consumption
- Base health of 150 HP
- Standard power consumption of 60kW
- Compatible with all vanilla science packs

#### Advanced Laboratory (R2)
- Enhanced research facility with improved capabilities
- 20x faster research speed compared to basic lab
- 1MW power consumption
- Supports all science packs including space science
- Increased health (500 HP)
- Requires research-speed-4 technology
- Custom visual design with improved aesthetics
- Specialized research capabilities for advanced technologies
- Enhanced module slots for better customization

#### Satisfactory Laboratory
- Specialized research facility inspired by Satisfactory
- Unique visual design with industrial aesthetics
- 350 HP durability
- Custom research capabilities
- Compatible with standard science packs
- Specialized for Satisfactory-inspired technologies
- Improved research efficiency for specific research paths
- Custom animations and visual effects

## Technology Categories

### Energy Technologies

#### Steam Power
- **Steam Energy R1**: Unlocks advanced boilers and steam engines (R2)
  - Requires steel processing
  - 100 science packs
  - 25 seconds research time
  - Unlocks boiler-r2 (3.6MW) and steam-engine-r2 (3.6MW)
  - Improved fuel efficiency compared to vanilla equipment
  - Enhanced visual design with industrial aesthetics

- **Steam Energy R2**: Unlocks elite boilers and steam engines (R3)
  - Requires Steam Energy R1
  - 500 science packs
  - 30 seconds research time
  - Unlocks boiler-r3 (7.2MW) and steam-engine-r3 (7.2MW)
  - Maximum fuel efficiency and power output
  - Advanced visual design with premium aesthetics

- **Steam Energy R3**: Unlocks oil-based power generation
  - Diesel fuel production (high-energy fuel)
  - Oil steam boiler (specialized for oil-based fuels)
  - Petroleum generator (converts petroleum gas to power)
  - Requires Steam Energy R2
  - 550 science packs
  - 30 seconds research time
  - Creates a new power generation path using oil products
  - Enables more efficient power generation in mid to late game

#### Solar Power
- **Solar Panel Equipment R2**: Advanced solar panel equipment
  - Requires solar panel equipment
  - 100 science packs
  - Enhanced power generation (120kW vs 60kW for vanilla)
  - Improved visual design
  - Better efficiency in low-light conditions
  - Reduced material cost compared to vanilla

### Power Distribution

#### Electric Poles
- **Electric Energy Poles R1**: Basic power distribution
  - Light pole R1 (7.5 tile coverage, 32kW capacity)
  - Medium pole R1 (9 tile coverage, 64kW capacity)
  - Long pole R1 (12 tile coverage, 128kW capacity)
  - Requires electric energy distribution 1
  - 150 science packs
  - 30 seconds research time
  - Improved visual design with industrial aesthetics
  - Better coverage and capacity than vanilla poles

- **Electric Energy Poles R2**: Advanced power distribution
  - Substation pole R1 (18 tile coverage, 256kW capacity)
  - Requires electric energy distribution 2
  - 110 science packs
  - 30 seconds research time
  - Enhanced visual design
  - Maximum coverage and capacity for power distribution
  - Specialized for large-scale power networks

### Storage Systems

#### Warehouse Technology
- Unlocks warehouse R1
- Requires steel processing
- 750 science packs
- 20 seconds research time
- Enables large-scale storage solutions
- Multiple access points for efficient logistics
- Custom visual design with industrial aesthetics
- Specialized for bulk storage of items and fluids

## Integration with RExtended

RETech is designed to work seamlessly with the RExtended mod, providing the necessary research paths to unlock and utilize the advanced machines and production chains introduced in RExtended. The integration includes:

- **Matching Technology Requirements**: All RExtended machines have corresponding technologies in RETech
- **Balanced Progression**: Research requirements are carefully balanced to create meaningful progression
- **Consistent Visual Style**: Technologies and research facilities share a consistent visual style with RExtended
- **Complementary Features**: RETech focuses on research while RExtended focuses on machines and production
- **Shared Resource Requirements**: Technologies use resources that are available through RExtended production chains

## Installation

1. Download the latest release from the mod portal or GitHub repository
2. Place the folder in your Factorio mods directory (typically `%appdata%/Factorio/mods` on Windows, `~/.factorio/mods` on Linux, or `~/Library/Application Support/factorio/mods` on macOS)
3. Enable the mod in Factorio's mod settings by checking the box next to "RETech" in the mod list
4. Start a new game or load an existing save to begin using the mod's features
5. For best experience, also install the RExtended mod

## Compatibility

- **Factorio 2.0**: Fully compatible with the latest version of Factorio
- **RExtended**: Designed to work seamlessly with the RExtended mod
- **Other Mods**: Compatible with most other mods that don't conflict with technology trees
- **Mod Combinations**: Works well with mods that add new resources or production chains
- **Save Compatibility**: Can be added to existing saves, though some technologies may be immediately available
